# Worked Examples

Three end-to-end examples show how to combine specialist skills with validation and handoff.

- [`board-deck-from-workbook.md`](board-deck-from-workbook.md) — Excel analysis to a board PowerPoint, including reconciliation and accessibility.
- [`policy-package.md`](policy-package.md) — Word policy from legal/HR inputs, plus accessible PDF and manager summary.
- [`inbox-and-calendar-triage.md`](inbox-and-calendar-triage.md) — Outlook triage and meeting preparation without sending or inviting without permission.

Use these to calibrate scope, evidence, and honesty. Each example includes what commonly goes wrong. Do not treat the fictional figures or policies as real.
