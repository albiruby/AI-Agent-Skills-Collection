# Worked Example: Policy Package in Word

## Scenario

HR asks: "Create a remote-work policy from these notes, legal comments, and our template. We need an editable DOCX and an accessible PDF for all staff."

## 1. Intake

- Audience: all employees; managers need a short approval checklist.
- Source: meeting notes, current draft, legal comments, HR template.
- Deliverable: DOCX, PDF, one-page manager summary.
- Constraints: legal wording must be preserved; brand template; accessible PDF; 6-8 pages.
- Authorization: create new files; do not edit the legal comments or original draft.

## 2. Inspect and reconcile

- Identify authoritative text: legal comments override earlier drafts where they conflict.
- List unresolved comments and decisions rather than silently choosing.
- Check terminology (employee/contractor, remote/hybrid), effective date, and jurisdiction.
- Mark placeholders for policy owner, review date, and local variations.

## 3. Structure

1. Purpose and scope
2. Definitions
3. Eligibility and approval
4. Working arrangements and hours
5. Equipment, security, and data handling
6. Health, safety, and workspace
7. Expenses and travel
8. Performance and review
9. Non-compliance and exceptions
10. Review and version history

## 4. Build the document

- Use the HR template's styles; do not manually bold headings.
- Apply Title/Heading 1-3, List Paragraph, Caption, and Quote styles.
- Insert a real table of contents and page numbers; update before export.
- Keep legal clauses as supplied; flag ambiguous wording instead of rewriting.
- Use tables for approval steps and responsibilities; repeat header rows.
- Add alt text to images; set document language.
- Add a one-page manager checklist as an appendix or separate brief.

## 5. Review

- Content review: facts, dates, roles, legal wording, cross-references.
- Consistency review: terminology, capitalization, numbering, tense.
- Accessibility review: headings, table headers, alt text, link text, reading order.
- Technical review: TOC updates, page breaks, table overflow, headers/footers.
- Export PDF; check tags, links, selectable text, and page layout.
- Run the accessibility checker where available; record what it flagged.

## 6. Handoff

- Deliver DOCX, PDF, and manager summary.
- State unresolved legal/policy questions and placeholders.
- Report validation performed and any checks that could not be completed.
- Note that legal approval is still required; the agent does not certify compliance.

## 7. What commonly goes wrong

- Rewriting legal text without authorization.
- Fake headings (bold paragraphs) that break navigation and accessibility.
- TOC that was inserted but never updated.
- Tables that split awkwardly or lose header rows.
- PDF exported before final pagination.
- Missing document language or alt text.
- Claiming policy approval when only drafting was requested.
