## Rules

- Record the command/action, not just the conclusion.
- Keep observed output short; link the full artifact.
- Redact tokens, cookies, personal data, and customer identifiers.
- Mark whether evidence is static, runtime, reproduced, or inferred.
- State what the evidence does **not** prove.
- Delete temporary artifacts according to the scope contract.
