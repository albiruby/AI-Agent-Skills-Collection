# Example 02 — Watering Plants (pour / liquid state change)

> Illustrative walk-through; no generation is claimed.

## Brief
After carrying the can, the woman waters the tomato plants. 6 seconds, medium shot.

## Gate A — Risk
Liquid that must change a state (soil darkens, can lightens) → **R3–R4**. Plan: prefer a rose-head droplet spray over a thin continuous stream; medium-wide framing; do not show the interior of the can; consider a hybrid insert if exact water behavior matters.

## Interaction spec (summary)
- Both hands or one hand on the top handle; the other hand may support the base (declare which).
- Beats: lift can to plant height → tilt gradually → spray begins only after the tilt → droplets arc downward → soil and leaves darken → tilt back → lower the can.
- Cues: counterbalance lean; water follows gravity; wet soil persists.

## Prompt
```
Medium-wide shot, static camera. The woman holds the green watering can by its top handle with her right hand and supports it with her left hand under the base. She tilts the can slowly toward the tomato plants. Only after the can tilts, water sprays in small droplets from the rose head, arcs downward onto the soil, and the soil darkens. She tilts the can back upright and lowers it.
Natural daylight, realistic style.
```

## Gate C — Checkpoints
T_tilt: can tilted, water not yet visible? → T_spray: spray from the spout end only? → T_land: droplets reach plants/soil, soil darkens? → T_end: soil stays wet, can upright, count one.

## Common failures
C9 (flows upward, appears before tilt, no wetting), D5 (wetness disappears), D2 (can duplicates), A6 (can hovers).

## Recovery
L3 simplify (one hand), L4 wider, L5 split into "tilt" and "result" inserts (soil darkening as a separate shot), L8 hybrid (stock water-on-soil insert or simulated droplets composited), L9 imply the pour via a cutaway to wet leaves.
