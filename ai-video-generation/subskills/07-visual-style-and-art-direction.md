# Subskill 07 — Visual Style and Art Direction

## Purpose
Translate a style reference or art direction into consistent visual language.

## Style breakdown
Specify relevant dimensions:
- Era, medium, genre, or reference tradition.
- Geometry and shape language.
- Texture scale and surface treatment.
- Shading and rendering.
- Color palette and contrast.
- Lighting quality and direction.
- Atmosphere, weather, and environmental effects.
- Image artifacts or imperfections.
- Level of realism or stylization.

## Positive direction first
Describe what the result should look like. Use exclusions only to prevent likely style drift.

## Style hierarchy
Separate:
- Global style: applies to the whole project.
- Scene style: applies to one location or shot.
- Subject style: applies to one character/object.
- Temporary effect: applies only at a specific moment.

## Consistency
Keep core style anchors stable across a sequence. Allow controlled variation in lighting, weather, palette, and composition when the brief calls for it.

## Style references
Use style references to guide rendering, not to unintentionally copy unrelated subjects or compositions. Do not treat a reference image as a universal instruction if it contains accidental details.

## Output
Provide a compact art-direction block with key visual anchors and likely style-drift exclusions.
