# Subskill 14 — Safety, Rights, and Originality

## Purpose
Keep video concepts and production within applicable safety, privacy, and rights boundaries.

## People and likeness
- Do not misrepresent a real person as doing or saying something they did not do.
- Obtain appropriate consent and rights for identifiable private individuals.
- Be especially careful with minors and sensitive contexts.
- Do not infer or depict sensitive personal attributes without a valid, user-authorized basis.

## Copyright and brand assets
- Do not assume that uploading a reference grants usage rights.
- Avoid copying protected characters, logos, distinctive franchise designs, or living artists' exact styles when not permitted.
- Offer high-level stylistic descriptors or original alternatives where appropriate.
- Use licensed or user-owned music, footage, images, fonts, and logos.
- Respect platform and model terms.

## Deceptive media
Consider whether the output could be mistaken for real footage or an authentic statement. Add appropriate disclosure or context where required by the platform, law, or use case.

## Safety
Do not create instructions or imagery that facilitate real-world harm, exploitation, or other disallowed activity. If a request is unsafe, do not attempt to route around the restriction with different wording.

## Output
Flag material rights, consent, disclosure, or safety concerns briefly and propose a compliant creative direction where possible.
