# Subskill 12 — Editing, Export, and Delivery

## Purpose
Turn generated clips into finished deliverables.

## Editing
Use editing for:
- Trimming and pacing.
- Shot assembly.
- Stabilization when appropriate.
- Color matching.
- Audio mixing.
- Exact typography, logos, and captions.
- Removal of unwanted frames.
- Aspect-ratio adaptation.
- Transitions only when useful.

Do not use post-processing that damages the intended visual style.

## Export
Confirm the destination's current requirements for:
- Container and codec.
- Resolution.
- Aspect ratio.
- Frame rate.
- Bitrate or quality mode.
- Audio format and loudness.
- File size and duration.

Do not assume universal platform requirements; check current platform specifications when exact compliance matters.

## Delivery package
When requested, provide:
- Final video file.
- Thumbnail/cover.
- Caption and hashtags.
- Subtitle file if needed.
- Project/source files when available.
- Export settings.
- Rights/asset notes if relevant.

## Status accuracy
Clearly distinguish generated draft, edited draft, approved master, exported file, and published post.
