# Subskill 02 — Concept and Story Design

## Purpose
Create concepts that are visually legible and achievable by the selected video model.

## Concept rules
- Build around one clear visual idea.
- Ensure the first moment communicates the situation quickly.
- Use a simple action, interaction, transformation, or reveal only when it serves the brief.
- Give the viewer a readable progression: setup → action/change → natural resolution.
- Do not force a twist, joke, conflict, or narrative arc when the user wants an observational moment.
- Avoid too many subjects, props, locations, and simultaneous actions.

## Action beats
For short clips, use 1–3 beats. Allocate time according to action complexity, not rigid percentages. Ensure the subject has enough time to complete the action.

For longer clips, break the concept into scenes or shots. Keep each shot's purpose clear.

## Model feasibility
Prefer actions with:
- Clear start and end states.
- Visible cause and effect.
- Limited hand-object contact.
- Few occlusions.
- Stable spatial relationships.
- Minimal simultaneous motion.

For complex ideas, simplify, split into multiple shots, or generate plates/assets separately.

## Output
Deliver a concept title, one-sentence premise, beat outline, and risk notes.

## Physics-aware concepting
Tier the idea before committing to it (subskill 15). If the concept depends on precise hand-object contact, liquids that change state, or mechanisms (R3–R4), propose a simpler, split, implied, or hybrid version alongside the original and explain the trade-off.
