# Subskill 09 — Audio, Dialogue, and On-Screen Text

## Purpose
Plan sound and text deliberately instead of allowing them to appear by accident.

## Audio layers
Consider:
- Dialogue.
- Voice-over/narration.
- Music.
- Sound effects.
- Environmental ambience.
- Silence.

Specify which layers are required, optional, or prohibited.

## Dialogue and lip sync
- Keep dialogue short and natural.
- Identify speaker and language.
- Avoid multiple simultaneous speakers unless needed.
- For exact wording, provide the precise line and verify whether the model can render it reliably.
- If lip sync is important, test it separately and inspect the result.

## Text and typography
AI video models may produce unreliable text. For exact logos, labels, UI, signs, captions, and legal copy, prefer post-production overlays or compositing when possible. If the model must render text, keep it short and verify every character.

## Audio consistency
Match ambience, reverb, perspective, and loudness to the scene. Avoid music or narration when the user wants a quiet observational clip.

## Rights
Use audio the user owns, has licensed, or is otherwise permitted to use. Do not assume that a generated or uploaded sound is cleared for every use.
