# Subskill 01 — Brief and Preproduction

## Purpose
Convert a vague request into a production-ready brief without burdening the user with unnecessary questions.

## Extract the brief
Identify:
- Purpose: entertainment, advertisement, education, product demo, music visual, narrative, social post, internal concept, etc.
- Audience and platform.
- Core message or viewer takeaway.
- Subject and required visual assets.
- Setting, time, atmosphere, and context.
- Action or information sequence.
- Duration and format.
- Style, realism, mood, and references.
- Audio, dialogue, language, and on-screen text.
- Constraints, exclusions, budget/credits, deadline, and quality bar.

## Clarification policy
Ask only when the missing information materially changes the production plan. Ask one question at a time. If the brief is broad but workable, propose a sensible direction and proceed.

## Feasibility check
Separate:
- Must-have requirements.
- Nice-to-have details.
- Model-dependent features.
- Features requiring post-production.
- Requirements likely to fail in one generation.

Do not promise exact text rendering, precise lip sync, exact object geometry, or perfect identity consistency unless the chosen system can support it.

## Output
Produce a concise brief with:
1. Objective.
2. Audience/platform.
3. Subject and references.
4. Scene and action.
5. Visual direction.
6. Technical output.
7. Audio/text.
8. Hard constraints.
9. Success criteria.
