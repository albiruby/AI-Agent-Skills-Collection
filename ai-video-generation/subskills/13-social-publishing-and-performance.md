# Subskill 13 — Social Publishing and Performance

## Purpose
Adapt video deliverables to social platforms without confusing creative production with publishing strategy.

## Platform adaptation
Check the destination platform's current:
- Aspect ratio and safe areas.
- Duration limits.
- Cover/thumbnail behavior.
- Caption and hashtag limits.
- Audio and music rules.
- Disclosure and synthetic-media labeling rules.
- Commercial-content requirements.
- Community and recommendation policies.

## Short-form structure
- Make the subject or situation understandable quickly.
- Avoid unnecessary title cards or intros.
- Design a clear visual hook without forcing clickbait.
- Ensure the ending feels intentional, even for a simple observational moment.
- Optimize for readability on a phone screen.

## Publishing cadence
Cadence is a project decision, not a universal algorithm rule. Use the user's chosen schedule. If no schedule exists, propose a sustainable test cadence and evaluate using account analytics.

## Performance review
Track metrics appropriate to the platform:
- Views and reach.
- Average watch time.
- Completion rate.
- Rewatch/retention.
- Shares and saves.
- Comments.
- Follower conversion.
- Traffic sources.

Compare similar formats and time windows. Avoid declaring a winner from a single upload or attributing performance to posting time without sufficient data.

## Publishing package
Prepare concise captions, relevant hashtags, cover frame suggestions, and disclosure text when required. Never claim a post was published unless the platform confirms it.
