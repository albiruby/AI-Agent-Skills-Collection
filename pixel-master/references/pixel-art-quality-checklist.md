# Pixel Art Quality Checklist

## Pixel craft
- [ ] Native resolution and pixel grid are correct.
- [ ] Silhouette is readable at native and target display size.
- [ ] Pixel clusters are intentional; no accidental noise or single-pixel debris.
- [ ] No unwanted banding, pillow shading, jaggies, or muddy anti-aliasing.
- [ ] Dithering and texture patterns support form rather than distract.

## Color and lighting
- [ ] Palette matches the approved palette/ramp rules.
- [ ] Value hierarchy reads in grayscale.
- [ ] Material ramps are distinct and lighting direction is consistent.
- [ ] Transparency and background contrast are correct.

## Composition/style
- [ ] Projection, scale, outline, and detail density match the style guide.
- [ ] Focal points and gameplay-critical elements are clear.
- [ ] Asset looks coherent beside representative project assets.

## Animation
- [ ] Canvas, pivot, and frame bounds are stable.
- [ ] No accidental frame pops, shimmer, clipping, or volume drift.
- [ ] Loop start/end and timing are correct.
- [ ] Tags, directions, and frame durations are correct.

## Tiles/UI/engine
- [ ] Tiles have no seams or missing adjacency combinations.
- [ ] UI text/icons are readable and states are distinct.
- [ ] Filtering, camera scale, sorting, and import settings are correct.
- [ ] No texture bleeding or subpixel jitter in test scene.

## Delivery
- [ ] Native sources retained where available.
- [ ] Exports and metadata match the manifest.
- [ ] Naming, dimensions, pivots, and licenses/provenance are documented.
- [ ] Archive opens and all expected files are present.
