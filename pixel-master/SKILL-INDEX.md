# Pixel Art Master Studio - Skill Index

**26 skills: 1 master orchestrator + 25 specialists.**

## Master Orchestrator

- `pixel-master` - Master orchestrator for end-to-end pixel-art production: art direction, characters, creatures, props, environments, tilesets, UI, typography, animation, VFX, atlases, automation, 3D-to-pixel, shaders, engine integration, QA, and delivery.

## A. Pixel Art Fundamentals & Art Direction

- `pixel-art-art-director` - Art direction specialist for pixel art projects.
- `pixel-art-fundamentals-specialist` - Specialist in core pixel art craftsmanship and pixel-level technique.
- `pixel-art-palette-color-specialist` - Color theory and limited palette specialist for pixel art.
- `pixel-style-conversion-specialist` - Specialist in translating concept art, photographs, high-res illustrations, and 3D renders into clean pixel art or pixel-style visuals.

## B. Character & Asset Creation

- `pixel-art-character-artist` - Specialist in pixel art character design and sprite creation.
- `pixel-art-creature-artist` - Specialist in pixel art monsters, animals, fantasy creatures, enemy variants, and boss sprites.
- `pixel-art-prop-item-artist` - Specialist in pixel art props, weapons, tools, furniture, food, potions, collectibles, inventory items, machines, and interactive world objects.
- `pixel-art-environment-artist` - Specialist in pixel art environments, backgrounds, landscapes, architecture, interiors, caves, atmospheric depth, and multi-layer parallax scenes.
- `pixel-art-tileset-level-artist` - Specialist in pixel art tilesets, seamless repeating tiles, autotiling bitmasks (47-tile blob, 16-tile Wang/marching squares), terrain transitions, modular level kits, and collision-aware level art.
- `pixel-art-isometric-artist` - Specialist in isometric pixel art using crisp 2:1 pixel projection, orthographic grids, isometric buildings, terrain blocks, props, depth sorting, and perspective consistency.
- `pixel-art-illustration-keyart-artist` - Specialist in large-canvas pixel art illustrations, key art, splash screens, game covers, promotional posters, character portraits, and cinematic pixel storytelling.

## C. UI, Icon & Typography

- `pixel-art-ui-icon-designer` - Specialist in pixel art UI design and iconography.
- `pixel-art-typography-logo-designer` - Specialist in pixel fonts, bitmap typography, custom pixel lettering, pixel logos, kerning, readability, text hierarchy, and logo animations.

## D. Animation & Visual Effects

- `pixel-art-animation-director` - Specialist in pixel art animation direction, motion design, timing, spacing, key poses, anticipation, follow-through, squash and stretch, arcs, frame budgets, and gameplay-synced loop planning.
- `pixel-art-sprite-animator` - Specialist in frame-by-frame pixel art sprite animation.
- `pixel-art-vfx-artist` - Specialist in pixel art visual effects (VFX) and sprite-based particles.

## E. Technical Art & Production Pipeline

- `pixel-art-spritesheet-atlas-architect` - Specialist in sprite sheet layouts, texture atlas packing, frame indexing, pivot/origin alignment, cell padding/extrusion, naming conventions, and structured engine metadata (JSON/XML/Aseprite hash/array).
- `pixel-art-editor-operator` - Cross-editor workflow specialist for pixel art software including Aseprite, LibreSprite, Pixelorama, Piskel, GraphicsGale, and Pro Motion NG.
- `pixel-art-procedural-automation-engineer` - Specialist in Python scripts, CLI batch processing, procedural pixel generation, automated palette quantization, sprite-sheet assembly, dithering pipelines, and reproducible image tooling.
- `pixel-art-3d-render-pipeline-specialist` - Specialist in 3D-to-pixel rendering pipelines.
- `pixel-art-shader-postprocess-specialist` - Specialist in pixel art shaders and post-processing effects.
- `pixel-art-game-engine-integration` - Specialist in integrating pixel art assets cleanly into game engines including Godot, Unity, GameMaker, RPG Maker, Defold, and Web/HTML5 Canvas.
- `pixel-art-optimization-performance` - Specialist in pixel art technical optimization and runtime performance.
- `pixel-art-qa-art-review` - Specialist in pixel-level Quality Assurance (QA) and Art Review.
- `pixel-art-production-delivery-manager` - Specialist in pixel art asset cataloging, folder architecture, naming conventions, versioning, production tracking, handoff specifications, documentation, and final delivery packaging.
